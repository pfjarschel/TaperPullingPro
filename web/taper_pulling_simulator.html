Model: the flame heats the fiber with a gaussian profile as wide as the flame; the elongation is shared along the fiber in proportion to the heating, and volume is conserved. The waist of a silica fiber in air guides a single mode at 1550 nm below 1.14 µm. Transmission: where the core stops guiding (waist near 40 µm), a fixed share of the light goes into four higher modes (HE12, TE01, TM01, HE21, as seen in a measured taper; it is not calculated from the shape). Each mode gathers phase along the simulated profile, with the exact modes of a silica rod in air, and is lost when the waist reaches its cutoff. Space bar: play or pause.
